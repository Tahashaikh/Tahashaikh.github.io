Contact

Get in touch

One inbox, read by a human. Use it for corrections, tool suggestions, or anything about how this site is run.

Email

hello@testverity.com

We usually reply within two to three business days.

What to include

To get a useful reply, add the page URL and a short description of the issue. For pricing corrections, a link to the vendor's current pricing page is the fastest way to get a fix published.

  • Corrections: page URL, what is wrong, and the source that confirms it.
  • Tool suggestions: the tool, the job it fits, and what it does better than the alternatives.
  • Advertising or partnership: tell us the brand and what you are proposing. We do not sell rankings or positive reviews.
  • Privacy requests: use the same address with "Privacy request" in the subject line.

What we cannot help with

We are a review publication, not a support desk. We cannot troubleshoot your account, recover your data, or contact a vendor on your behalf. For product problems, use the vendor's own support channel — it will be faster.

Editorial policy

Review requests are considered, but paid placement is not offered. For the full standards, read how we evaluate.
