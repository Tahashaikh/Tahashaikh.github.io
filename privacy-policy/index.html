Privacy Policy

Last updated: 24 October 2026

This policy explains what information Test Verity ("we", "us") collects when you visit testverity.com, how we use it, and the choices you have. We keep the site deliberately simple: no account system, no comment database, and no tracking pixels beyond what advertising and basic hosting require.

Information we collect

We do not ask you to create an account, and we do not sell personal information. We may process:

  • Information you send us. If you email us, we receive your address and whatever you include in the message. We keep it only as long as needed to answer.
  • Technical information. Our host and advertising partners may log your IP address, browser type, device type, referring page, and the pages you visit. This is standard server and ad-serving data.
  • Cookie and advertising data. Described in the next section.

Cookies

Cookies are small text files stored by your browser. We use them for two purposes: to let our advertising partner serve and measure ads, and to remember consent choices where required. You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings; the site will still work, but ads may be less relevant.

Google AdSense and third-party advertising

We use Google AdSense to display advertising. Google and its partners use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this and other websites.

  • Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to you based on your visit to this site and/or other sites on the internet.
  • Google uses the DoubleClick DART cookie for this purpose. You may opt out of personalised advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings .
  • You can also opt out of third-party vendor cookies for personalised advertising at aboutads.info/choices and youronlinechoices.eu (EU/UK).
  • For details on how Google uses data from partner sites, read How Google uses information from sites that use its services .

Advertising partners may include Google and its certified ad technology providers. Where required by law, a consent message is shown before non-essential cookies are set, and you can change your choice at any time through that message or your browser settings.

Analytics

We prefer privacy-friendly measurement. If we use an analytics service, we choose one that does not build cross-site profiles or place advertising cookies. Any aggregate statistics we see are anonymised counts such as page views and referrers.

Legal bases and your rights (GDPR / UK GDPR)

Where the GDPR applies, we rely on legitimate interests for operating and securing the site, consent for personalised advertising and non-essential cookies, and consent or legitimate interests for answering your emails. You have the right to access, correct, delete, restrict, or object to processing of your personal data, and to withdraw consent at any time. To exercise these rights, email hello@testverity.com. You may also complain to your local data protection authority.

Your rights in California (CCPA/CPRA)

California residents may request to know what personal information is collected, request deletion, and opt out of the sale or sharing of personal information. We do not sell personal information. Advertising cookies may count as "sharing" under the CPRA; use the opt-out links above or email us to exercise your rights.

Data retention

Emails are kept for as long as needed to resolve the matter and maintain a record of corrections. Server and advertising logs are retained according to our host's and Google's own retention schedules.

Children

This site is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has sent us personal data, contact us and we will delete it.

International transfers

Our host and advertising partners operate globally, so data may be processed outside your country. Transfers rely on the safeguards those providers publish, such as standard contractual clauses.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the law or our tools change. The date at the top always shows the current version. Material changes will be highlighted on this page.

Contact

Privacy questions and requests: hello@testverity.com.
