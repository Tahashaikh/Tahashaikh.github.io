What the estimate assumes

  • One fix per affected test per year.
  • Maintenance time means locator repair, re-runs, and the review around them.
  • The reduction is the share of that work you expect to remove, not a vendor guarantee.

A real suite also carries environment, data, and assertion maintenance. This tool covers the locator and flake slice because that is the part self-healing and AI repair claim to reduce.

Use it before a vendor demo

Take the current annual cost into the demo. Ask the vendor what share of that number their tool actually removes, and what review time it adds back. If the answer is vague, the number is marketing.
